Book Recommendations

Bored of your bookshelf? Discover your next big reading milestone with our curated list of unforgettable adventures!

🎒 Adventure 🎒
Wildborn (Spirit Animals) by Brandon Mull
The Explorer by Katherine Rundell
Hatchet by Gary Paulsen
The Boxcar Children by G. Chandler Warner
The Bletchley Riddle by R. Sepetys & S. Sheinkin
⭐ Alisha's Pick
Hatchet by Gary Paulsen

A gripping survival story that keeps you on the edge of your seat. Paulsen's beautiful prose captures both the struggle and wonder of nature in a way that will make you think about resilience long after you finish the book.

🚀 Science Fiction 🚀
The Wild Robot by Peter Brown
A Wrinkle in Time by Madeleine L'Engle
The Last Last-Day-of-Summer by Lamar Giles
The Giver by Lois Lowry
The First State of Being by Erin Entrada Kelly
⭐ Alisha's Pick
A Wrinkle in Time by Madeleine L'Engle

A timeless classic that blends adventure with profound philosophical questions. This imaginative journey through dimensions and galaxies explores themes of courage and self-acceptance that resonate with readers of all ages.

📜 History 📜
Code Name: Verity by Elizabeth Wein
Wolf by Wolf by Ava E. Reid
The Book Thief by Markus Zusak
Crispin: The Cross of Lead by Avi
Chains by Laurie Halse Anderson
⭐ Alisha's Pick
Code Name: Verity by Elizabeth Wein

An absolutely stunning historical novel about female pilots in World War II. Told through letters and coded messages, this intricate narrative builds to a powerful conclusion that explores loyalty, sacrifice, and the strength found in friendship.

🔍 Mystery 🔍
The Wollstonecraft Detective Agency by Jordan Stratford
Truly Devious by Maureen Johnson
A Good Girl's Guide to Murder by Holly Jackson
The Mysterious Benedict Society by Trenton Stewart
One of Us Is Lying by Karen M. McManus
⭐ Alisha's Pick
A Good Girl's Guide to Murder by Holly Jackson

A cleverly constructed mystery where nothing is quite what it seems. With engaging characters and a protagonist who won't give up, this page-turner keeps you guessing until the very end. Perfect for mystery lovers!

🌟 Realistic Fiction 🌟
Wonder by R.J. Palacio
The Hate U Give by Angie Thomas
The Fault in Our Stars by John Green
Refugee by Alan Gratz
The Sky is Everywhere by Jandy Nelson
⭐ Alisha's Pick
Wonder by R.J. Palacio

A heartwarming story about kindness and acceptance that will touch your soul. Through multiple perspectives, Palacio beautifully illustrates how one person's courage can inspire an entire community to be kinder and more understanding.
